# The Professor (persona spec)

An original, fictional character. He is not based on, and must never resemble, any real person.

## Name

**Professor Sportington III**, host of The Athletics Archive.

## Look

- Illustrated style: ink line art with warm watercolor wash, like a vintage magazine illustration. Never photorealistic.
- Late 60s, round wire spectacles, tidy white mustache, tweed jacket with elbow patches, bow tie in the channel accent color.
- Props: a wooden pointer, a stopwatch, a battered leather notebook of box scores.
- Home set: a film-room archive with a chalkboard of X's and O's, an old projector, unmarked trophies and pennants with no lettering.

## Behavior in video

- Role: he "replays the tape", setting up the moment, pausing at the turning point and revealing the number that shows how unlikely it was.
- Long-form: appears in the cold open, at chapter resets (about every 2–3 minutes), at the big reveal and the close. Play diagrams, charts and photos carry the rest.
- Shorts: a 1–2 second cameo at most; the hook frame belongs to the payoff.
- Motion: none. Still poses only, per the owner's no-zoom, no-movement direction (2026-11-02).

## Voice

- American English, male, warm baritone, measured pace (~150 words per minute in long-form).
- Kokoro TTS voices to audition in Phase 3: `am_michael`, `am_adam`, `am_eric`, `am_onyx`.
- Catchphrase options (use sparingly, not every video): "Let's check the record." / "Footnote time."

## Reference set to generate once (Phase 3)

Front, three-quarter and profile; expressions neutral, amused, surprised, skeptical; poses pointing, reading notes, leaning on desk. Stored in the series Style Bible and reused for consistency.

## Disclosure decision

Clearly illustrated and non-realistic, so YouTube's AI-disclosure setting is not required for him ([YouTube help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491)). The pipeline still records this decision per video.
