Why does a loss ruin your whole night? Why do you say "we" when your team wins?

Game Day Brain explains what sports really do to your brain and body, for fans and for players. Each video is a countdown of signs and reasons, built on real research: what scientists found, who they studied, and where the evidence is still unsettled.

You'll find the psychology of fandom, rivalries and superstitions, the science of pressure, sleep, pain and the hot hand, and why home-field advantage is real.

Illustrations are made with AI, and the narration is a synthetic voice. The research isn't: every study is named in the video's description.
