You've seen the highlight. Here's what actually happened, and why it almost didn't.

The Athletics Archive rebuilds the biggest moments in sports history, one moment per video: great games, record-breaking performances and impossible upsets. Professor Sportington III, our illustrated and entirely fictional host, replays the tape with the box score in hand, using charts, running totals and the numbers that show how unlikely it really was.

No countdown lists. No hot takes. Every number is either sourced or marked as our own math, and the sources are in each video's description.
